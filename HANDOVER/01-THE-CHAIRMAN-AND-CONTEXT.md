## His network — the most valuable asset in the project

### Akif Saeed — sitting Chairman of the SECP

The **Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan** is the securities and
non-bank financial regulator. Akif Saeed is its serving Chairman: ex-Commissioner
Securities Market, ex-Asian Development Bank, ex-American Express corporate
banking.

He is **mentoring Taha**. He is *not* an investor and should never be described
as one. Two meetings so far:

**Meeting 1 — 23 July 2026, 09:30.** His stated number-one concern is **default
risk** — specifically an early recipient who takes the pot and stops paying.

**Meeting 2 — 23 July 2026.** He was impressed, called Halqa "original," and
mentioned that the Oraan founders had also come to him. He gave concrete product
direction which **supersedes several of the project's earlier self-imposed
absolutes**:

- Do not worry about licensing, insurance or the TASDEEQ bureau partnership for
  now — he waved these off as solvable.
- Investigate linking to a **licensed online AMC plus a trustee** so Halqa never
  holds money: **CDC** (Central Depository Company), mutual funds, margin
  trading, and Pakistan's first digital AMC (likely **Mahaana Wealth** — verify).
- **Revive the Vault** (previously scrapped): a savings buffer such that a member
  with money in it cannot default, because a miss draws from their own holding.
- Asked whether **auto-debit truly works**; hinted that **Raast is getting a
  proper auto-debit feature** — verify against the SBP roadmap.
- The **SECP Regulatory Sandbox** can work for Halqa.
- **Halqa should cover defaults on its own balance sheet and insure that exposure
  with takaful.** This reversed the earlier "no company capital at risk" rule.
- **Filtering over enforcement** (his ex-boss's philosophy): do not build recovery
  enforcement; build so there is no incentive to default. Practically — show
  members only what they are eligible for, income-tiered circles, and keep
  barriers to entry low.
- **Separate consumers from savers.** Savers go to the vault and AMC investment;
  consumers who take the pot early to spend get personal guarantees and stronger
  terms.
- **Study Money Fellows and Egypt** — what regulation allowed it to exist, its
  regulator, its credit system, its default history. He noted **Oraan was not
  SECP-licensed** and he is unsure how it legally operates.
- **He asked for a full product report in about two weeks** covering every aspect
  from cybersecurity to marketing, and Taha must be able to answer any question
  on it. *(This deliverable drove the report set in `11`.)*

Ex-Alfalah SEVP, ex-Rozee CEO, microfinance bank director. Liked the idea, is not
investing (he has left the finance sector). His feedback shaped the product
direction materially:
