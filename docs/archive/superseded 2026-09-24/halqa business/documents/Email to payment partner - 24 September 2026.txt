WHAT THE PLATFORM DOES

Members of a committee each contribute a fixed amount on a fixed schedule. On
each cycle one member, or on the daily product a small group of members,
collects that cycle's contributions, each paid to them directly by a payer.
The platform records the arrangement and initiates collection. It does not
hold member funds at any point, and no amount passes through an account of
its own.

The decisive consequence is that a collection taken from member A has to settle
to member B, who is the recipient for that cycle. The platform is the initiator
of the transaction but is never the beneficiary of it. Separately, and on the
same debit, a service fee settles to the platform's own account and a third
component settles to a licensed takaful operator's account.
