    RAAST P2M REQUEST TO PAY - MERCHANT GUIDE
    FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

    A) Raast P2M refers to Pakistan's Real-time Interbank Settlement Mechanism for Person-to-Merchant transactions. Raast is a digital payment system introduced by the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) to facilitate instant, round-the-clock electronic fund transfers between banks and financial institutions in Pakistan.

    "P2M" stands for Person-to-Merchant, indicating transactions from individuals (peers) to merchants or businesses. Raast P2M allows Consumers to make payments directly to merchants through a real-time and interoperable payment system, enabling faster and more efficient digital transactions.

    A) Request-to-Pay (RTP), allowing merchants to send payment requests to customers, who can pay instantly or schedule payments for later using their mobile banking apps. Payments are processed securely via IBAN-based channels, reducing transaction costs compared to traditional methods. Raast P2M promotes financial inclusion and convenience while fostering a cashless economy in Pakistan

    A) Any customer with a Bank Alfalah account and an active mobile banking app can use the Raast P2M service. Merchants registered with Raast can send payment requests to their customers.

    A) Merchants who are onboarded on Raast P2M can initiate a Request to Pay transaction. Just ask Merchant to follow the following Steps:

    • Log in to your Alfa Business App
    • Go to the top right button to open features
    • Go to Request to Pay
    • Initiate a Transaction either through IBAN or Raast.

    A) All Raast-enabled banking apps can accept Raast P2M Request to Pay transactions. Just ask the customer to follow the following steps if the customer is an ALFA user,

    • Customer will receive a notification pop-up on their phone
    • Log in to your Alfa app
    • Press More
    • Go to Payment and Authorization
    • Go to Requests
    • Accept the Payment
    • Get Transaction Payment Receipt

    Overview

    Raast P2M QR, launched by the State Bank of Pakistan, is the country’s first instant person-to-merchant payment system. Integrated with the Alfa Business App, it enables individuals and businesses to accept seamless digital payments instantly and securely.

    What you get

    • Accept customer payments through a Raast-enabled QR code.
    • Instant fund transfers directly into the merchant’s account.
    • Easy registration through the Alfa Business App or assisted onboarding.
    • Convenient, cashless transactions that reduce handling costs.

    With Raast P2M QR, merchants and customers benefit from fast, transparent, and reliable payments—supporting the shift towards a cashless economy. Terms and conditions apply.

    Features & Benefits

    Universal Access

    Available across all Raast-enabled banking applications.

    Direct Transfers

    Payments settled directly into the merchant’s International Bank Account Number (IBAN).

    QR-Based Payments

    Customers can pay instantly by scanning static or dynamic QR codes.

    Account-Based Security

    Only account-to-account transactions, ensuring safe payments.

    Quick Settlement

    T+1 settlement cycle for all Raast P2M transactions.

    Cost Efficiency

    Lower merchant discount rate (MDR) for affordable acceptance.

    Easy Customer Payments

    Customers log in to their Raast-enabled app, scan, pay, and receive instant receipts.

    Simple Merchant onboarding

    Register through the Alfa Business App or via assisted onboarding for larger organisations.
