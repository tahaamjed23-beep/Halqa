Source: Islamabad Capital Territory Administration, Vehicle Transfer, https://ictadministration.gov.pk/vehicle-transfer/, read 25 September 2026.

To transfer ownership of a vehicle, already registered with Excise and Taxation, Islamabad, you are required to follow the below-mentioned procedure.
Documents Required
Following documents are required along with the application:
Application for transfer of ownership address to Motor Registration Authority (MRA) / Excise & Taxation Officer (ETO), Islamabad
Photocopy of CNIC of the Applicant
Filled and signed Form 'F'
Photocopy of NIC of the Seller
Computerized Transfer Letter
Transfer of Ownership Form
In case of a vehicle leased from a bank or a leasing company, NOC / Transfer Letter is required from relevant bank or leasing company
An affidavit to the effect that the vehicle is neither stolen nor required in any other case and that the applicant would be himself responsible for any discrepancy pointed afterward
Dealing Authority: Director (Excise & Taxation)
Processing Fees: Rs/- 1200-3000(In NBP)
Processing Time: 04 Weeks
