Code of Civil Procedure 1908, Order XXXVII, as reproduced in Justice Shabbir Ahmed, Working paper on Suits under Summary Procedure, Sindh Judicial Academy (https://www.sja.gos.pk/assets/publication/JusticeShabbir/Working%20paper%20on%20Suits%20under%20Summary%20Procedure.pdf), read 25 September 2026.
1. Application of Order. This Order shall apply only to the High Court, the District court, and any other Civil Court notified in this behalf by High Court.
2. Institution of summary suits upon bills of exchange, etc.,(1) All suits upon bills of exchange, hundies or promissory notes, may, in case the plaintiff desires to proceed hereunder, be instituted by presenting a plaint in the form prescribed.
3. Defendant showing defence on merits to have leave to appear. (1) The court shall upon application by the defendant, give leave to appear and to defend the suit, upon affidavits which disclose such facts as would make it incumbent on the holder to prove consideration, or such other facts as the court may deem sufficient to support the application.
