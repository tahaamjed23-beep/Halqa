Bank customers can use Raast service for sending or receiving funds using their IBANs even if they do not have a Raast ID. FEATURES MAKING LIFE EASIER Free of Cost No charges on Raast related services. FAST Instant payments into accounts whether it is a wallet or account or branchless banking.
Sending funds via Raast is possible even without sender registering for Raast ID. Step 1: Access your bank's provided channel (mobile application, internet banking, branch over the counter, USSD). Step 2: Specify beneficiary's IBAN or Raast ID (mobile number) on Raast-enabled banks. Step 3: Confirm the amount to be sent within limits provided by your bank. Step 4: Your payment will be transferred instantaneously.
