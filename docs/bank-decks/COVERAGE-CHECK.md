# Coverage check: Mashreq presentation v3 against all of Halqa

Checked on 29 September 2026 against three sources: the 81 slide "Halqa Complete Position
29 September 2026", the 21 slide "Halqa and Mashreq Bank Pakistan" pitch of 28 September,
and MASTER-CONTEXT (decisions D1 to D16 and the product record).

## Verdict

v3 covered the core story but not all of Halqa. It left out the turn market and points
bought with money (D9) and never asked the bank for the credit reporting route (D11). It
also called the direct debit mandate an existing Mashreq product, which it is not, and
used internal terms an outsider cannot read. The new decks fix all of these.

## What was wrong or unclear in v3, and the fix

1. **Direct debit mandate.** v3 said "Existing Mashreq products at every step". Mashreq's
   published products do not include a debit mandate (MASTER-CONTEXT 12.3); it is one of
   the requests. The new slide says the mandate is the one new piece.
2. **Credit reporting.** Non-negotiable under D11, but missing from v3's six requests.
   It is now request 6.
3. **Internal codes in sources.** Codes like HQ-CP-05, HQ-MF-05 and HQ-RS-01 mean nothing
   to a bank. They are replaced by plain names.
4. **Terms left undefined.** "Checks level 1, 2, 3", "early turns earned", "names match
   0.90", "balance share" and "Unknown" are now written in plain words.
5. **Oraan fee row.** "Same fee for every turn: up to 21% a month" read as a contradiction.
   It now reads "Price of an early turn: up to 21% of the instalment a month".
6. **Growth slide.** It had a ring diagram and a list of channels with no numbers. It is
   removed. The concrete parts moved to slide 9 (accounts opened 6 to 20 at a time) and
   slide 20 (circles per host).
7. **Problem slide.** The waffle chart drew a count of 117 committees as if it were a
   share. It is replaced by a diagram of the informal money route with its failure points.
8. **Dated figures.** Removed "US$18 average instalment, 2013" and "26% financially
   literate".
9. **No summary.** An audience that knows nothing got no overview. Slide 2 adds one.
10. **Look.** Full lime slides, a white box behind the closing logo and inherited shape
    shadows are gone. The palette is lime on white, and every chart has direct labels.

## Left out on purpose

- Messages (WhatsApp, Roman Urdu, voice notes), later products (gold, credit builder,
  seasonal and employer circles), the legal map and the design history. These are in the
  81 slide reference.
- The fixed cost breakdown by role. The rebuild you asked for on 25 September is paused,
  so only totals are shown.

## Raqami deck: what changed from Mashreq

- A Shariah Structure slide: qard for instalments, ujrah for the fee, mudarabah for the
  waiting balance, takaful for cover, penalties to charity.
- Raqami's own products: the 7 day term deposit (mudarabah), Saving Pots, free cash
  deposits at over 700 Askari Bank branches, free transfers, and EFU as its takaful
  partner.
- UAE family circles removed: Raqami opens accounts for residents only.
- Evidence led by Hakbah; Why Now adds the 1 January 2028 deadline to end riba.
- **Your decision needed (open question Q6).** For Raqami I showed turns as swaps with no
  price and points as earned only, never bought. Turn sales and points bought with money
  cannot run inside a fully Islamic bank. Nothing is removed from Halqa itself; the
  features are just not offered through Raqami. Change this if you want another route.
- **Not used:** Raqami's logo. Downloading it still needs your approval (Q8). Its name is
  set in type and shown in charcoal instead of a brand colour.
- **Unknown:** whether Raqami offers automatic debits. The deck presents it as a request.
